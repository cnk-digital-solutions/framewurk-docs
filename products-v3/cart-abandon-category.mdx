A **product category for cart abandon** is the entity a [Cart Abandon Overlay](/plugins/cart-abandon-overlay) points at. It is a [product category](/products-v3/product-category) cut down to what the overlay needs, plus the copy the overlay shows on top of the product detail: a label, a heading and an optional description. It is the first entity of the **Products: V3** admin section, listed as **CUSTOM: Product category for cart abandon**.

## What the overlay takes from it

| Part of the overlay | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| Highlight label | **Offer label** |
| Heading | **Offer name**, with its `#price#` replaced by the price as the price line shows it |
| Price line | The primary price written with the **Price expression**, with the full price struck through in front of it when there is one. Left out when **Hide price line** is ticked |
| Description | **Offer description**, or the **Product description (overlay)** of the product detail when it is blank, with its `#price#` replaced by the price as the price line shows it |
| Follow-up sentence | The **Followup price expression** written with the follow-up price, at the description's `#followup_price_sentence#` placeholder or after the description |
| Accordions and the rest of the content | The **Single product view** content of the product detail |
| Accept button | **Product URL** |

The product detail is found through the copy tag, as for a product category: the one for the visitor's country, then the one whose country is **(Not set)**. Its product name, packshot and label settings are not used by the overlay.

## Identity

- **Name** is internal. It is what the **Product category for cart abandon** dropdown of the Cart Abandon Overlay and the admin lists show.
- **Copy tag** connects the entity to its product details.

## Copy

- **Offer label** is the highlight label at the top of the overlay, shown in capitals as white text on black. It starts as **Exclusive offer**. Leave it blank for an overlay without a label.
- **Offer name** is the heading of the overlay, used instead of the product name from the product detail. `#price#` in it is replaced by the price as the price line shows it: the full price struck through, then the primary price. It accepts the responsive line breaks and non-breaking spaces used elsewhere in the CMS, and `[b]` and `[/b]` around the part to show in bold. For example `Get [b]Weekend Print + Premium Digital[/b] [br-m][br-l][br-xl]for the price of [br-xs][br-s][b]Premium Digital[/b]` breaks after "Premium Digital" on desktop and after "for the price of" on phones.
- **Offer description** replaces the **Product description (overlay)** of the product detail when it is set. It is rich text: bold, italic, underline and links. `#price#` in it is replaced by the price as the price line shows it, as in the heading. When it is blank, the product detail's text is shown. Either text can place the follow-up sentence with a `#followup_price_sentence#` placeholder, see [Pricing](#pricing).
- **Hide price line** leaves the price line out of the overlay: the heading is followed by the description. The price can still appear in the heading or the description through `#price#`. The primary price is still required, since the overlay only opens when one resolves for the visitor's country.

## Pricing

An entity has the three price slots of a [product category](/products-v3/product-category#pricing): the **Primary price** (required), the **Full price**, struck through in front of the primary price when set, and the **Followup price**. Each slot names up to three sources, tried in the same order as on a product category: via Charge Group, via Offer API, then the price code. The **Price expression** writes the price line and carries the period, so `#price# for your first year` gives the struck-through full price, the primary price and then "for your first year". Period conversion and rounding work as described for a [product category](/products-v3/product-category#period-rounding-and-savings).

The **Followup price expression** writes the follow-up sentence when a follow-up price resolves for the visitor's country: `#price#` is the follow-up price and `#price_primary#` the primary price. The sentence replaces the `#followup_price_sentence#` placeholder in the description when there is one, and otherwise follows the description as a paragraph of its own. Without a follow-up price the placeholder is removed. A follow-up price resolves only when a **Followup price expression** is set: follow-up sources without one yield no follow-up price. **Require followup price**, when ticked, keeps the overlay from opening when no follow-up price resolves; leave it unticked when the follow-up price is optional information.

The **Product Categories with inactive pricing** list in **Prices: Offer API** covers these entities too.

## Product URL and tracking

**Product URL** is the link behind the accept button. The overlay appends `countryCode=` with the visitor's country, then every query parameter of the page URL, so campaign parameters reach the checkout.

The **Campaign codes and tracking** fieldset, collapsed by default, holds **Offer id**, the three **Default campaign code** fields and **Is trial**. The overlay uses **Offer id** as the tracking label of the accept button and does not read the other fields.

## When the overlay can show

The overlay fetches the entity for the visitor's country after the page renders. It can open only when a product detail exists for the copy tag, for the country or as the **(Not set)** fallback, a primary price resolves for the country, and a follow-up price resolves too if **Require followup price** is ticked. Otherwise it never opens, and in edit mode the preview button reads **Cart abandon not available**, see [the Cart Abandon Overlay](/plugins/cart-abandon-overlay#in-edit-mode).

## The admin list

The list shows the name, copy tag, charge group, price code and Offer API price code of each entity, with the same filters, search and **Clone** link as the product categories. A clone shares the copy tag, so it shares the product details too.

## Dependencies

The rules of a [product category](/products-v3/product-category#dependencies) apply: a copy tag or price expression in use cannot be deleted, and deleting a charge group or a follow-up expression clears that field. Deleting a product category for cart abandon does not touch the Cart Abandon Overlays that pointed at it: their overlay never opens, and in edit mode the preview button reads **Cart abandon not available**.
