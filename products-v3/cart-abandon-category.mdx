A **product category for cart abandon** is the entity a [Cart Abandon Overlay](/plugins/cart-abandon-overlay) points at. It is a [product category](/products-v3/product-category) cut down to what the overlay needs, plus the copy the overlay shows on top of the product detail: a label, a heading and an optional description. It is the first entity of the **Products: V3** admin section, listed as **CUSTOM: Product category for cart abandon**.

## What the overlay takes from it

| Part of the overlay | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| Highlight label | **Offer label** |
| Heading | **Offer name**, with its `#price#` replaced by the price as the price line shows it |
| Price line | The primary price written with the **Price expression**, with the full price struck through in front of it when there is one. Left out when **Hide price line** is ticked |
| Description | **Offer description**, or the **Product description (single product)** of the product detail when it is blank, with its `#price#` replaced by the price as the price line shows it |
| Follow-up sentence | The **Followup price expression** written with the follow-up price, at the description's `#followup_price_sentence#` placeholder or after the description |
| The content under the description | The **Single product view** content of the product detail, see [in the Cart Abandon Overlay](/products-v3/product-detail#in-the-cart-abandon-overlay) |
| Accept button | **Product URL** |

The [product detail](/products-v3/product-detail) is found through the copy tag, as for a [product category](/products-v3/product-category).

## Identity

- **Name** is internal. It is what the **Product category for cart abandon** dropdown of the Cart Abandon Overlay and the admin lists show.
- **Copy tag** connects the entity to its product details.

## Copy

- **Offer label** is the highlight label at the top of the overlay, shown in capitals as white text on black. It starts as **Exclusive offer**. Leave it blank for an overlay without a label.
- **Offer name** is the heading of the overlay, used instead of the product name from the product detail. `#price#` in it is replaced by the price as the price line shows it: the full price struck through, then the primary price.
- **Offer description** replaces the **Product description (single product)** of the product detail when it is set. `#price#` in it is replaced by the price as the price line shows it, as in the heading. When it is blank, the product detail's text is shown. Either text can place the follow-up sentence with a `#followup_price_sentence#` placeholder, see [Pricing](#pricing).
- **Hide price line** leaves the price line out of the overlay: the heading is followed by the description. The price can still appear in the heading or the description through `#price#`. The primary price is still required, since the overlay only opens when one resolves for the visitor's country.

## Pricing

Pricing works as on a [product category](/products-v3/product-category#pricing): the same three price slots, price sources, period, rounding and expressions. The differences are in where the prices show:

- The primary price, with the full price struck through in front of it, is the price line of the overlay and the `#price#` of the heading and the description.
- The follow-up price is written as a sentence by the **Followup price expression**. The sentence replaces the `#followup_price_sentence#` placeholder in the description when there is one, and otherwise follows the description as a paragraph of its own. Without a follow-up price the placeholder is removed.
- **Require followup price**, when ticked, keeps the overlay from opening when no follow-up price resolves.

## Product URL and tracking

**Product URL** and the **Campaign codes and tracking** fields work as on a [product category](/products-v3/product-category#product-url-and-tracking), with these differences:

- **Product URL** is the link behind the accept button.
- **Offer id** is the tracking label of the accept button, and no plugin setting overrides it.
- **Is trial** is not read.

## When the overlay can show

The overlay fetches the entity for the visitor's country after the page renders. It can open only when a product detail exists for the copy tag, for the country or as the **(Not set)** fallback, a primary price resolves for the country, and a follow-up price resolves too if **Require followup price** is ticked. Otherwise it never opens, and in edit mode the preview button reads **Cart abandon not available**, see [the Cart Abandon Overlay](/plugins/cart-abandon-overlay#in-edit-mode).

## The admin list

The list shows the name, copy tag, charge group, price code and Offer API price code of each entity, with the same filters, search and **Clone** link as the product categories. A clone shares the copy tag, so it shares the product details too. Each row also has a **Preview** link, see [Preview](#preview).

## Preview

**Preview** shows the overlay of this entity without a page and without a trigger. It is a link next to **Clone** on each row of the list and a button next to **History** on the entity's own page.

The page has one frame with the overlay open in it. The overlay stays open: the cross, the dismiss button and the Escape key do nothing there. The accept button opens the **Product URL** in a new tab. The button texts are **Yes please** and **No thanks**, because the texts of a page's overlay are set on the [Cart Abandon Overlay](/plugins/cart-abandon-overlay), not on this entity.

The **Breakpoint** buttons and the **Country** select work as in the [product category preview](/products-v3/product-category#preview). The overlay fills the screen it is on, so the height of the frame is part of what you see: drag the handle under the frame to change it.

When the overlay would not open in the chosen country, the frame says **Not available in** that country and gives the reason, one of the conditions of [When the overlay can show](#when-the-overlay-can-show).

## Dependencies

The rules of a [product category](/products-v3/product-category#dependencies) apply: a copy tag or price expression in use cannot be deleted, and deleting a charge group or a follow-up expression clears that field. Deleting a product category for cart abandon does not touch the Cart Abandon Overlays that pointed at it: their overlay never opens, and in edit mode the preview button reads **Cart abandon not available**.
