**Banner Row** is the only child of the [Banner Section](/plugins/banner-section). It has no settings of its own: it holds up to three banners and decides how they share the width at each breakpoint. A row can mix [Banner (Variant 1)](/plugins/banner-section/variant-1) and [Banner (Variant 2)](/plugins/banner-section/variant-2) freely.

A row renders its **first three banners only**. A fourth banner stays in the structure board but is not on the page.

## Layout per breakpoint

How many banners the row holds decides the layout. The thresholds are the page breakpoints: M starts at 740px, L at 1024px.

| Banners in the row | Below 740px (S and XS) | M (740 to 1023px) | L and XL (1024px and up) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| One | A full-width card | Full width, image beside the content | Full width, image beside the content |
| Two | Two cards, one below the other | Half the row each, image above the content | Half the row each, image beside the content |
| Three | Three cards, one below the other | **One per row**, each laid out like a single banner | A third of the row each, image above the content |

Below M every banner is a card on its own row, in structure board order, so a row of three is simply three cards. At M a row of three is deliberately not split: three columns would be too tight, so each banner takes the full width and the single-banner layout. Its heading keeps the size it has in thirds, not the single banner's larger one. A row of one in the same section follows suit at M: its banner's heading drops to that size too, so the section reads as one size. In a section without a row of three the single banner keeps its larger heading.

"Image beside the content" and "image above the content" describe Variant 1. Variant 2 always stacks its graphic above its CTA from M up; its cards behave the same as Variant 1 cards below that. See the variant pages for the details.

## Height: the tallest banner wins

From M up the banners in a row share one height, the height of the tallest of them, and the shorter ones stretch to match. Each banner's **Vertical alignment** setting (**Top**, **Center** or **Bottom**, default **Center**) decides where its content sits inside the stretched box. It is set per breakpoint: the XL choice holds for the smaller breakpoints until you tick one and choose there. Below M the cards stack, so each is as tall as its own content.

## Width of the images

The width of a banner's image depends on this layout: a Variant 1 image is sized per layout and breakpoint, a Variant 2 graphic per breakpoint by its own setting. See [how Variant 1 images are sized](/plugins/banner-section/variant-1#how-the-image-is-sized) and [the Variant 2 foreground image](/plugins/banner-section/variant-2#the-foreground-image).
