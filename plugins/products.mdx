The **Products** widget is the offers section of a V3 page. It shows a row of product cards, each with a packshot, name, price, intro text, a **Select** button and a **View details** button that opens a full-screen overlay. Above the cards sits a sticky navigation bar with **Subscribe**, **Compare** and **Why the FT** tabs, which can be switched off to show the cards alone.

It is a root-level widget for V3 pages and comes in two variants that share the same settings:

| Variant | Children | Use it when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Products (Ungrouped)** | [Product](/plugins/products/product) | All products belong together in one row |
| **Products (Grouped)** | [Product Group](/plugins/products/product-group), each holding Products | Products fall into named families (for example digital, print, professional) and visitors should be able to filter by family |

Products are not authored on the page. Each **Product** child points at a **product category** managed in the Product Manager admin, and the name, price, packshot, intro text and overlay content come from there when the page loads, for the visitor's country.

## Adding a Products widget

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the parent">
    In structure mode, add **Products (Ungrouped)** or **Products (Grouped)** (module **Custom Widgets**) at the root of the page content.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the children">
    Ungrouped: add one **Product** per offer, in the order the cards should appear.

    Grouped: add a **Product Group** per family, then the **Product** children inside each group.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the settings">
    Every setting has a default that works. The ones most often changed are the **Compare section** and the **Why the FT** tab, described below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Settings

Both variants have the same form, arranged in the following groups, with one exception: the **FT Professional mobile link** fieldset exists only on **Products (Grouped)**, the only variant that renders that link.

### General

- **Product overlay trigger CTA text** is the label of the secondary button on every card (default **View details**, up to 15 characters). It also serves as the analytics label of that button.
- **Product tab bottom padding** is **Default** or **Reduced** and applies at M, L and XL. Use **Reduced** when a banner follows the Products section directly.

### Navigation configuration

- **Show navigation** (on by default) controls the sticky bar with the **Subscribe**, **Compare** and **Why the FT** tabs. Untick it to show the product cards alone: the bar is not rendered, and the **Compare section** and **Why the FT** settings disappear from the form and are reset to their defaults when you save. The three subscription check fields disappear and reset as well, because that link lives in the bar.
- **Subscription check text** and **Subscription check link** define the "Check if your organisation subscribes" link at the right-hand end of the navigation bar, in both variants. Both must be set for the link to appear.
- **Subscription check link mobile position** places that link on narrow screens (below 600px), where the bar has no room for it: **Below navigation** (the default) is a full-width strip directly under the bar; **After the first product** is a tile among the product cards, right after the first one. With a single product the tile setting is ignored and the strip is used. See [where the links appear](#where-the-links-appear).

### Compare section

- **Show Compare section** turns the **Compare** tab on or off. Even when on, the tab only appears once there are at least two plans to compare.
- **Comparison table heading** and **Comparison table subheading** are shown above the table.
- **Product category group** widens the choice of plans in the comparison dropdowns. Without it, visitors can only compare the products present in this section. With a group selected, the product categories in that group (managed in the Product Manager admin under *Product category groups (for comparison page dropdowns)*) are offered as well.

See [the comparison table](#the-comparison-table) for how the table behaves.

### FT Professional mobile link configuration

Only on **Products (Grouped)**. The three fields define the "Two or more readers? Explore Professional" link: a preamble, the link text and the link itself. All three must be filled in for the link to show. It is only shown on mobile, in the group dropdown row, see [where the links appear](#where-the-links-appear).

### Why the FT

**Why the FT tab** has three modes:

| Mode | What the tab does |
| --- | --- |
| **Do not show** | No tab |
| **Link out** | The tab is a link to **Why the FT? link**, opened in a new window |
| **Preset** | The tab reveals a preset inside the Products section, as a third panel next to Subscribe and Compare |

In **Preset** mode, choose the preset with the preset picker. Its content is fetched when the page loads and shown in place when the tab is clicked. A preset can be used only once per page: saving a second Products section (or a Dynamic Preset plugin) with the same preset fails with "This preset is already present on the page".

## Behaviour

### What visitors see

The section fetches its products after the page has rendered and shows a skeleton placeholder in the meantime. Each product is fetched for the visitor's country:

- A product that is not available in the visitor's country, or that has no price or product detail, is **hidden**. The remaining cards close up.
- In the grouped variant, a group with no available products is hidden entirely, together with its heading and its entry in the group dropdown.
- If no product in the section is available, the section shows **No offers found**.

### Cards per row

| Breakpoint | Layout |
| --- | --- |
| L and XL (1024px and up) | Four cards per row, with packshot and intro text |
| M (740 to 1023px) | Two cards per row, with packshot and intro text |
| S and XS (below 740px) | One card per row, no packshot, no intro text. Below 600px the **Select** button is hidden: tapping the card or its three-dot button opens the overlay, where the **Select** button lives |

### The navigation bar

The navigation bar is sticky and holds up to three tabs:

- **Subscribe** shows the product cards and is always present.
- **Compare** shows the comparison table, when **Show Compare section** is on and there are at least two plans to compare.
- **Why the FT** follows the **Why the FT tab** setting.

If the visitor has scrolled past the top of the section, switching tabs scrolls back up to it.

With **Show navigation** off there is no bar and no tabs: the section shows the product cards only. In the grouped variant the group dropdown row then sticks to the top of the viewport instead of below the bar.

### Where the links appear

| Link | Ungrouped | Grouped |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Subscription check | Right-hand end of the navigation bar; below 600px a strip under the bar or a tile after the first product, per **Subscription check link mobile position** | Same as Ungrouped |
| FT Professional (mobile) | Not shown | Right-hand end of the group dropdown row, mobile only |

The group dropdown row is only rendered when at least two groups have available products, so a grouped section with a single visible group shows no FT Professional link.

Switching **Show navigation** off removes the subscription check link in both variants; its fields are hidden and reset.

### The product overlay

**View details** opens a full-screen overlay. It shows the product's packshot, name, price and intro text, a **Select** button, and the content configured for the product in the Product Manager (the *Single product view* plugins). A scrollable menu across the top lists every available product in the section, across all groups, so visitors can move between plans without closing the overlay.

### The comparison table

The **Compare** tab shows up to three plans side by side (two below 600px), each chosen from a dropdown. The candidates are the available products in this section plus, optionally, those of the **Product category group**. In the grouped variant the dropdowns are organised by product type: digital, print, print + digital.

The first columns are pre-filled. Use **Pre-select in comparison table** on a [Product](/plugins/products/product#pre-selecting-in-the-comparison-table) to control which plans they are; otherwise the first candidates are used.

The rows of the table (features, ticks and values) come from the feature configuration of each product in the Product Manager, not from the page.

## URL parameters

The section reacts to these query parameters on the page URL:

| Parameter | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| `show_comparison=true` | Opens the **Compare** tab on load |
| `show_comparison=scroll` | Opens the **Compare** tab and scrolls to the section |
| `show_wtft=true` | Opens the **Why the FT** tab on load (preset mode only; any value works) |
| `show_product_overlay=<slug>` | Opens the overlay of the Product whose **Slug for activating overlay on page load** matches |
| `show_group=<slug>` | Pre-selects the [Product Group](/plugins/products/product-group) whose **Slug** matches (Products (Grouped) only) |

Every other query parameter on the page URL is appended to the **Select** links of the products, so campaign parameters reach the checkout.

## Controlling other plugins from the navigation

Switching tab in the navigation bar is a page-wide event, not just a change inside the section. Content elsewhere on the page can fold away and come back with the tabs, and links elsewhere on the page can switch the tab.

### Folding content on Compare or Why the FT

Wrap the content in a [Collapsible Content Container](/plugins/collapsible-content-container). It has two settings, **Collapse when 'Compare' tab is activated** and **Collapse when 'Why the FT' tab is activated**.

Every tab switch checks every container on the page, above or below the section: the containers ticked for the tab that opened fold to zero height with a short animation, and all of them unfold on **Subscribe**. The typical use is the hero above the Products section in a container ticked for **Why the FT**, so that the preset content starts right under the page header instead of below the hero.

The containers follow the tab however it was opened: a click in the bar, a [URL parameter](#url-parameters) or an [in-page link](#switching-the-tab-from-elsewhere-on-the-page). A tab opened by URL parameter is applied once the products have loaded, so the content shows for a moment before it folds.

### The mobile heading

**Products Heading (Mobile)** is a heading line for phones (below 740px). Placed inside **Products (Ungrouped)**, it sits under the navigation bar and shows on **Subscribe** only: **Compare** and **Why the FT** hide it, with nothing to set. Placed at the root of the page, it stays whatever the tab. To make it fold with the hero, put it inside the hero's Collapsible Content Container, before or after the hero.

### Switching the tab from elsewhere on the page

Every V3 plugin with an **Action** on its link, for example a Hero Banner slide, a Banner, a Teasers item or a Button, offers three actions that target the Products section:

| Action | What the click does |
| --- | --- |
| **Scroll to the first Product section's Subscribe tab** | Opens **Subscribe** and scrolls to the section |
| **Scroll to the first Product section's Compare tab** | Opens **Compare** and scrolls to the section |
| **Scroll to the first Product section's Why the FT tab** | Opens **Why the FT**; scrolls to the section only if the visitor has scrolled past its top |

The link acts on the first Products section of the page and leaves the page URL unchanged. Collapsible Content Containers react as they do to a click in the bar. Use these actions only for tabs the section shows: **Compare** needs **Show Compare section** on and at least two plans, **Why the FT** needs the **Preset** mode. On a page without a Products section the link does nothing.
