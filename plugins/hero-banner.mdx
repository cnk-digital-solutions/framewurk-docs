The **Hero Banner** is a root-level widget for V3 pages. It shows one slide at a time across the full width of the page and, on desktop, rotates through its slides automatically. Each slide is a child plugin, and two kinds of slide are available:

| Child plugin | What it shows | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Slide** | Two lines of heading text, a CTA button and a graphic on a slate background | [Slide](/plugins/hero-banner/slide) |
| **Slide (video)** | A looping, muted Vimeo background video, optionally linked | [Slide (video)](/plugins/hero-banner/slide-video) |

Both kinds can sit under the same Hero Banner in any order.

## Adding a Hero Banner

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the parent">
    In structure mode, add **Hero Banner** (module **Custom Widgets**) at the root of the page content.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the dwell time">
    **Slide dwell time** is how long each slide stays on screen before the carousel moves on, in seconds (5 to 1800, default 10). It applies to every slide, including video slides.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add slides">
    Add one or more **Slide** or **Slide (video)** children. The first child is the one visitors see first, and on small screens it is the only one they see.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The two kinds of slide

- [**Slide**](/plugins/hero-banner/slide) is the standard slide: heading text, a CTA and a graphic, with a fixed height per breakpoint (364 px at L and XL, 240 px at M, 136 px at S and XS).
- [**Slide (video)**](/plugins/hero-banner/slide-video) plays a Vimeo background video and derives its height from one of three sizing methods.

## Behaviour

### Desktop: a carousel

At L and XL (1024 px and wider) the Hero Banner is a carousel:

- Slides fade from one to the next after the dwell time.
- When there is more than one slide, a row of dots and a play/pause button appear in the bottom-left corner. Clicking a dot activates that slide and restarts the timer. Pause stops the rotation until play is pressed again.
- Only the active slide is clickable.

### Below desktop: the first slide only

At M, S and XS (below 1024 px) there is no carousel. The **first slide is shown on its own** and the other slides and the controls are not displayed. Put the slide you care about most first.

### Height: the tallest slide wins

Slides are stacked on top of each other, so the Hero Banner takes the height of its tallest slide and every slide stretches to fill it:

- On desktop, all slides count.
- Below desktop, only the first slide counts, because it is the only one shown.

Image slides contribute their [fixed height](/plugins/hero-banner/slide#layout-per-breakpoint). Video slides contribute a height that depends on their sizing method, which can be taller or shorter than an image slide. See [how video slides behave next to other slides](/plugins/hero-banner/slide-video#mixing-video-slides-with-other-slides) for what happens to a video slide that is shorter than its tallest sibling.

<Tip>
  If a Hero Banner mixes image and video slides, the height is recalculated on every resize from each slide's configuration, never from what is currently on screen, so a slide that was stretched to match a taller sibling never influences the next calculation.
</Tip>
