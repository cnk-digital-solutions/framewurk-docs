**API-driven Teaser Item** is a child of the [Teasers](/plugins/teasers) widget. Where a [Teaser Item](/plugins/teasers/item) is one fixed card, this plugin expands into the **latest articles from an FT news stream** — up to 4 of them — at its position among the other cards.

Use it to mix live content with hand-picked cards: for example two manual teasers followed by the two newest articles from a stream. If the whole widget should track a single stream, tick **API driven** on the [parent](/plugins/teasers#fully-api-driven-mode) instead.

## Setting it up

- Paste a **News Stream URL** (an ft.com stream page, for example `https://www.ft.com/global-economy`). The **Concept ID** is looked up automatically when you leave the field; enter it by hand if the lookup fails.
- **Number of articles to show** caps the injected cards at 1 to 4.

## What the cards look like

Each injected article renders exactly like a manual card: the article's image, topic and title, with the heading linking to the article on FT.com in a new window.

The articles are refreshed in the background roughly every 1 hour — see [article freshness](/plugins/teasers#article-freshness).
