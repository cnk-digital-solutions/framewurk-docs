**Product** is the child that puts one offer into a [Products](/plugins/products) widget. It sits directly under **Products (No groups)**, or inside a [Product Group](/plugins/products/product-group) under **Products (Grouped)**. Either way the plugin itself holds no copy: it points at a **product category**, and the card is built from that category when the page loads.

## Choosing the product category

**Product category** is the only required field. The dropdown lists every product category defined in the Product Manager admin (there is a link to it under the field). The category determines, per country:

- the product name, packshot, label and intro text (the product detail),
- the price and any follow-up price (the price codes),
- the link behind **Select** (the product URL and offer ID),
- the content of the **View details** overlay and the rows of the comparison table.

If the category has no valid price or product detail for the visitor's country, the card is not shown.

## Pre-selecting in the comparison table

**Pre-select in comparison table** puts this product into the **First slot** or **Second slot** of the comparison table when the **Compare** tab opens. If several products in the section claim the same slot, the first one in page order wins. Products without a slot fill the remaining pre-filled columns in the default order: digital plans first, then print, then print + digital.

## Opening the overlay from a link

**Slug for activating overlay on page load** lets a link open the page with this product's overlay already open: give the product a slug, then link to the page with `?show_product_overlay=<slug>`. If several products share a slug, the first available one on the page is shown.

## Analytics

**data-trackable override** replaces the offer ID in the `data-trackable` attribute of the **Select** button, on the card and in the overlay. Leave it blank to track by offer ID.
