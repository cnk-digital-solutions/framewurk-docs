**Product Group** is the child of **Products (Grouped)**, and the only plugin that can sit directly under it. Each group is a heading with an optional description, followed by its own row of [Product](/plugins/products/product) cards. It is not available under **Products (No groups)**.

Use groups when the offers on a page fall into families that visitors would want to browse separately, for example digital, print and professional plans. When all offers belong together, use **Products (No groups)** and skip this level.

## Settings

- **Heading** is shown on the page above the group's cards.
- **Dropdown label** is optional. It is used for the group's entry in the group dropdown; when empty, the heading is used there as well. Set it when the on-page heading is too long for a dropdown.
- **Description** is a short rich-text lead under the heading. Bold, italic, underline and links are available, along with the responsive line-break and non-breaking-space tags used elsewhere. The field is pre-filled with placeholder text, so replace or clear it.

## Behaviour

### The group dropdown

When at least two groups have available products, a sticky dropdown row appears below the navigation bar with **All plans** and one entry per group. **All plans** shows every group in page order, each under its heading; choosing a group shows that group alone. The subscription check link and the FT Professional mobile link sit at the right-hand end of this row, see [where the links appear](/plugins/products#where-the-links-appear).

With only one group visible there is no dropdown row, and the section looks like the ungrouped variant.

### Headings on small screens

Group headings and descriptions are shown from M (740px) upwards. On phones they are not displayed, and the dropdown is how visitors tell the groups apart.

### Groups with no products

A group is hidden, including its heading and its dropdown entry, when none of its products is available in the visitor's country. See [what visitors see](/plugins/products#what-visitors-see).
