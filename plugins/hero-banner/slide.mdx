**Slide** is the standard child of the [Hero Banner](/plugins/hero-banner). It has a fixed layout and a fixed height per breakpoint, so a Hero Banner made only of these slides never changes size between slides.

## Layout per breakpoint

The slide adapts to the viewport in three steps. Heading text is always a single line per text line: text that does not fit is cut off with an ellipsis rather than wrapped.

| Breakpoint | Slide height | What is shown |
| --- | --- | --- |
| L and XL (1024 px and up) | 364 px | Heading left-aligned in the left half, CTA button below it, graphic in the right half |
| M (740 to 1023 px) | 240 px | Heading only, centred, at the larger size. No CTA, no graphic |
| S and XS (below 740 px) | 136 px | Heading only, centred, at the smaller size. No CTA, no graphic |

Below L the Hero Banner shows its first slide only, so on tablets and phones a Slide is effectively a heading on a slate background. The CTA is now shown.

## The graphic

The **Raster image** is placed in the right half of the slide at L and XL, anchored to the bottom-left corner of that half and displayed 364 px tall, which is the full slide height.

- Upload a **1240 x 728 px PNG**.
- PNG is expected because transparent areas show the slate background of the slide, which lets the graphic sit on the banner without a visible box.
- The image is cropped to the 1240 x 728 frame if its proportions differ, using the subject location set on the file in the media library.

## The CTA

The CTA is always rendered at L and XL. The **CTA label** doubles as the button's analytics label.

## Next to video slides

A Slide has the fixed heights above, so when it shares a Hero Banner with a [Slide (video)](/plugins/hero-banner/slide-video) whose sizing method produces a taller slide, the Hero Banner grows to the video slide's height and the Slide stretches with it: the heading and CTA stay top-left in the taller box and the graphic stays anchored to the bottom-left of its half. See [mixing video slides with other slides](/plugins/hero-banner/slide-video#mixing-video-slides-with-other-slides) for the other direction.
