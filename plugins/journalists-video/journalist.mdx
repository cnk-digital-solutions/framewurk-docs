**Journalist** is the child of [Journalists (Video)](/plugins/journalists-video) and the only plugin that can sit under it. Each one is a tile of the carousel.

## Poster image

The **Poster image** fills the tile until the video plays, and again when the visitor leaves the tile or the video ends.

- An image with other proportions than the recommended ones is cropped to the tile, around the focal point set on the image in the media library.
- The name and job title that visitors see on a tile are **part of the poster image**. The tile draws no text over the poster, so add them to the image itself.
- Use a frame of the video, or a picture with the same framing, so the change from poster to video is not a jump.

## Video

Produce the video in portrait, in the proportions of the tile (9:16). A landscape video is shown in full inside the tile, with black bands above and below it.

The tile hides Vimeo's own controls. Visitors use the tile's buttons, see [Playing a video](/plugins/journalists-video#playing-a-video).

## The information card

**Name**, **Job title** and **Text** are what the [information card](/plugins/journalists-video#the-information-card) shows. **Name** is also read out with the tile's buttons by screen readers, and **Name** and **Job title** are the alternative text of the poster.

The card has a link under the text only when an **Action** is chosen.
